Thyroid secretory capacity — T4 supply to the body

NHANES 2007–2012 population reference (n = 8,039 euthyroid adults): SPINA-GD median 44.9 nmol/s [IQR 39.7–50.4] · SPINA-GT median 2.16 pmol/s [IQR 1.68–2.82].
In 545 L-T4-treated adults, SPINA-GD was 23.8% lower (d = 1.037, p = 2.6×10⁻¹²⁷) while SPINA-GT was preserved (d = 0.195), localising the therapeutic deficit to peripheral T4→T3 conversion. The deficit deepens with age: 10% in those <40 years, 17% at 40–59, 25% at ≥60.
